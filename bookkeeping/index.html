Frequently Asked Questions

What bookkeeping services does Propel Group offer?

Monthly bookkeeping and close, bank, credit card, and loan reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, financial reporting (P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow), catch-up and cleanup of past periods, and a tax-ready handoff to your CPA at year end.

My books are months behind. Can you help?

Yes. Our Cleanup service is a one-time project that brings past-due books current, reconciles your accounts, and transitions you smoothly to monthly service.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Every engagement is quoted based on transaction volume and complexity. There are three levels of support: Essentials, Growth, and Cleanup. Email info@propelgroup.ai for a quote.

Do you work with my CPA?

Yes. We deliver organized, reconciled books to your CPA at year end so tax season is never a scramble.

How does onboarding work?

Review (we assess where your books stand today), Onboard (connect accounts and set up your chart of accounts), Clean Up (bring past periods current), and Close Monthly (reconciled books and reports every month).
