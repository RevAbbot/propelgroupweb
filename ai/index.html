Frequently Asked Questions

What does Propel Group's business consulting cover?

Strategy that aligns initiatives with your business objectives, governance and ethics guardrails for safe AI adoption, implementation from proof of concept to production, process and workflow automation, and training for leaders and teams.

How does Propel Group keep AI use safe and compliant?

We set up approved, secure tools that keep proprietary data protected, check output so results are accurate and trusted, and build governance and compliance frameworks with the EU AI Act and evolving global regulations in mind.

What are the steps in a consulting engagement?

Audit (assess data readiness and risk posture), Strategy (define high-ROI use cases), Governance (put ethics and compliance frameworks in place), and Scale (deploy and monitor across the business).

How are results defined?

Every engagement starts with clarity on three points: the specific problem being solved, how it will be solved, and the expected result you can see and measure. Engagements focus on growing income or cutting the cost of repetitive work.

Can consulting be combined with bookkeeping?

Yes. Reliable financial data is the foundation for any AI initiative, so Propel Group also offers monthly bookkeeping, and the two services are designed to work together.
