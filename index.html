Frequently Asked Questions

What does Propel Group do?

Propel Group, LLC offers two services built to work together: business consulting that streamlines processes and workflows, including AI and automation, and monthly bookkeeping that keeps your finances accurate, current, and ready for decisions.

Who does Propel Group work with?

Growing companies that want faster, more reliable workflows, cleaner books, or both. Propel Group is based in the San Francisco Bay Area and works with clients remotely worldwide.

Can I hire Propel Group for just one service?

Yes. You can work with us on business consulting, bookkeeping, or both. Clean books make better automation possible, and automation helps keep books clean, so many clients use both.

How does an engagement with Propel Group start?

In four steps: a conversation about where the friction is, an assessment of your workflows, books, or both, a plan with a clear scope, timeline, and fee, and then an ongoing partnership with regular check-ins and reporting.
